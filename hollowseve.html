Hollow's Eve

A Tower Defense Tale

On the eve of All Hallows, the veil between worlds grows thin. From a crack in the graveyard earth, the Pumpkin King has torn open a portal and summoned his monstrous court.

Three cursed grounds stand between the mortal world and his throne. Each is a labyrinth; each is hungrier than the last. Your towers grow in power only as you survive — new weapons unlock at waves 3, 5, 7, 9, and 11.

Hold the line until dawn. Guard the Jack-o'-Lantern. Do not let the night win.

  • Tap a tower tile at the bottom, then tap a grassy cell on the field to build.
  • New towers unlock as you survive waves — you start with three.
  • Enemies march along the dirt path toward your Jack-o'-Lantern. Every leak costs a life.
  • Earn 🍬 candy by destroying enemies. Spend it on more towers and upgrades.
  • Every tower earns XP for kills and can be promoted to higher ranks with permanent bonuses.
  • Tap any placed tower to open its panel — upgrade up to level 5 or sell for 60% refund.
  • Beat a maze to unlock the next one. Progress is saved.
  • Drag to orbit the camera, pinch or scroll to zoom. Use ⏩ speed to fast-forward waves.
